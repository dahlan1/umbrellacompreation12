Umbrella Corporation - B.O.W Monsters RE1 to RE8

Tyrant T-002 (RE1)
The first successful Tyrant prototype designed by Umbrella as a bio-weapon. Stands about 2.5 meters tall with immense raw strength. Notorious for its heart exposure and ability to mutate further when damaged.
Hunter α (MA-121) (RE1)
A reptilian B.O.W combining human and reptile DNA, resulting in enhanced agility and deadly claws. Roughly 2 meters tall, this creature is fast and lethal, capable of decapitating humans with a single swipe.
Chimera (RE1)
A grotesque fusion of human and insect DNA, able to crawl on ceilings and walls with great agility. Small but deadly, typically around 1.5 meters long, with sharp claws and the ability to spit acidic venom.
Yawn (RE1)
A giant snake mutated by the T-Virus, exceeding 6 meters in length. Aggressive and territorial, often encountered in dark underground areas. It retains the constricting abilities of normal snakes but with enhanced strength.
Neptune (RE1)
A great white shark exposed to the T-Virus, growing larger and more aggressive. Predatory and fast, it poses a lethal threat in aquatic environments. Known for its massive size and ability to breach water surfaces with tremendous force.
Tyrant T-103 (Mr. X) (RE2)
A mass-produced Tyrant sent to retrieve the G-Virus. Towering at about 3 meters, Mr. X relentlessly pursues targets with great strength and intelligence. Notable for its trench coat and ability to withstand massive damage.
Licker (RE2)
A mutation from further T-Virus exposure, featuring exposed brains and a long tongue. Agile and fast, it attacks silently in dark corridors. Approximately 2 meters long with claws capable of tearing through metal.
G (William Birkin) (RE2)
Scientist William Birkin injected himself with the G-Virus, transforming into a grotesque and powerful creature. His mutations progress through stages, each more monstrous than the last, with increasing size and additional appendages.
Ivy (RE2)
Plant-based B.O.W created by T-Virus experiments. Uses tentacle-like appendages for attack and defense. Agile and dangerous, often found in greenhouse areas. Releases toxic spores that can poison victims.
Nemesis-T Type (RE3)
A Tyrant variant with enhanced intelligence and regenerative powers. Tasked with eliminating S.T.A.R.S. members. Stands about 3 meters tall and can wield weapons. Notable for its iconic "STARS..." vocalization.
Hunter β (RE3)
Successor to the α Hunter, with improved speed and ferocity. Equipped with sharp claws and enhanced senses. Stalks survivors in Raccoon City. Agile enough to dodge gunfire and leap long distances.
Cerberus (RE3)
Zombie dogs infected by the T-Virus, fast and vicious. Commonly deployed as guard animals by Umbrella. Small but lethal, attack in groups. Highly aggressive with enhanced bite force capable of crushing bones.
Garrador (RE4)
A blind but heavily armored creature with deadly claws. Uses echolocation to hunt. Approximately 2.5 meters tall and extremely dangerous in close quarters. Vulnerable to loud noises that disrupt its hearing.
El Gigante (RE4)
Massive giant infected by Las Plagas parasite. Standing over 4 meters tall with immense strength. Slow but devastatingly powerful. Often used as living siege weapons to break through fortifications.
Verdugo (RE4)
Elite assassin infected by Las Plagas, wearing a gas mask. Fast and deadly with lethal claws. About 2 meters tall and incredibly agile. Guards key locations and pursues targets relentlessly.
Regenerador (RE4)
A terrifying creature that can regenerate limbs rapidly, infected by Las Plagas. Difficult to kill without specific chemical agents. 2 meters tall with disturbing breathing sounds that signal its presence.
Uroboros Aheri (RE5)
A parasitic B.O.W that causes massive mutations and size increase. Known for incredible strength and regenerative abilities. Over 3 meters tall, with tentacle-like appendages that can crush or impale targets.
Majini (RE5)
Infected humans controlled by the Uroboros virus. Vary in size and power but generally aggressive and hostile. Equipped with firearms and melee weapons. Some can mutate further when damaged.
Wesker (RE5)
Albert Wesker enhanced by viruses, possessing superhuman speed, strength, and regeneration. A major antagonist. Stands about 1.9 meters tall. Deadly in combat with enhanced reflexes and tactical intelligence.
J'avo (RE6)
Humans infected with the C-Virus, capable of rapid mutation and regeneration. Varying in form and strength, some exhibit advanced combat abilities. Can mutate damaged body parts into weapons.
Ustanak (RE6)
A massive bio-organic weapon deployed to hunt survivors. Over 3 meters tall, heavily armored and armed with mechanical appendages. Relentless and nearly unstoppable, with a single-minded pursuit of targets.
Molded (RE7)
Creatures formed from bio-organic mold infection. Varied in shape but mostly humanoid, with grotesque features. Aggressive and can overwhelm survivors with numbers. Approximately 2 meters tall with sharp claws.
Jack Baker (RE7)
Patriarch of the Baker family, infected and enhanced by bio-organic mold. Possesses immense strength and regeneration. Serves as a major antagonist throughout the game. Approximately 2 meters tall with incredible durability.
Moroaica (RE8)
Mutated insect-like B.O.W found in the village. Small but vicious, known for fast attacks and swarm behavior. Represents infection spread by Mother Miranda's experiments. Approximately 1.5 meters tall with sharp appendages.
Lady Dimitrescu (RE8)
Tall vampire-like mutant with enhanced speed, strength, and regeneration. Over 2.9 meters tall. One of the major bosses of RE8, infamous for her height and clawed hands. Can transform into a dragon-like creature.
